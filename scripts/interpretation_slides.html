A heatmap colour is relative to its own row.

37 genes × 94 saliva samples. Red = above that gene's mean; blue = below it; white = near it.

3SPAG9

Follow the bottom row. A visible pattern does not tell you its adjusted p-value.

The row z-score equals normalized log2 expression minus the gene's across-sample mean, divided by its across-sample standard deviation. A z-score of plus two is two standard deviations above that mean, not twofold expression. The colour scale is limited to minus three through plus three. Rows are ordered by absolute moderated t-statistic, not clustered; columns preserve the input order. The original image has no class-annotation strip. Outlines do not imply clusters or statistical intervals.

03 / decode the axes and both thresholds

Left is effect direction. Up is statistical evidence.

Each dot is a tested gene. The full-universe plot keeps the non-selected genes visible.

Horizontal / magnitude and direction

log2 fold change = cancer minus control.

−1 = half the expression; −2 = one-quarter. Zero means no estimated average difference.

Vertical / evidence after many tests

−log10(BH-adjusted p).

1.30 ≈ padj 0.05; height 2 = padj 0.01. Higher does not mean a larger effect.

Selection / an AND rule

|log2FC| > 1 AND padj < 0.05.

A high grey dot near the centre can have a small adjusted p-value but insufficient fold change.

Benjamini-Hochberg adjustment addresses multiple gene tests. A gene-specific adjusted p-value is not the probability that the gene is a false discovery or that a patient has cancer. The plotted test uses mean normalized log2 expression differences, converted to fold changes by exponentiation with base two. A twofold biological threshold and a statistical threshold answer different questions. The asymmetric cloud is a feature of this computed dataset; do not assume there must be significant genes on both sides.

06 / combine the views, keep the limits

A coherent expression signal is not a diagnostic test.

What these figures support

  • Heatmap: individual variability and shared relative-expression patterns among selected genes.
  • Volcano: estimated cancer-control effects and adjusted statistical evidence.
  • Saved saliva result: 37 downregulated DEGs; all 14 published genes recovered, plus 23 additional genes.

What they do not establish

  • Red = cancer, or every cancer sample below every control.
  • Causality, exact numerical replication, or biological importance from a p-value alone.
  • Diagnostic sensitivity, specificity or AUC, or independent clinical validation.

Selection matters: these genes were chosen for differences in this same cohort. A visually striking heatmap is not independent validation.

Saliva DE verdict: partially reproduced. This lesson concerns these saved saliva figures; it does not assess the status of other cohorts, model runs or ongoing robustness checks.

The selected heatmap genes come from full-cohort differential expression, so the apparent pattern is partly expected by construction. Establishing predictive usefulness requires leakage-controlled evaluation and independent validation, not visual separation alone. Differences in gene mapping, filtering and the number of selected genes prevent an exact replication claim. Do not copy the older saliva snapshot's remaining-work list as the current status of the entire project; this deck deliberately limits its claim to interpreting these figures.

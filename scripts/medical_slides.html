01 / Clinical contextPartial reproduction / medical draft

A non-invasive sample. A translational challenge.

Gastric cancer (GC): can salivary transcriptomics support earlier detection?

From gastric mucosa to a saliva-based liquid biopsy.Accessible sampling is attractive; diagnostic validity must still be established.

Why this paper matters

Lopes et al. compare GC-associated tissue and saliva expression, then evaluate whether saliva requires its own diagnostic signature.

What reproduction asks

Do independent preprocessing and statistical analysis recover the signals, and do prediction claims survive validation safeguards?

Lopes et al., Scientific Reports 15, 18491 (2025). Raw processing, DE and GO completed; modeling and robustness ongoing.STAGE

Set the clinical stage before introducing computational methods. Endoscopy and histopathology remain central to GC diagnosis; this paper does not establish their replacement. Saliva is an accessible liquid-biopsy specimen, but tissue expression and salivary expression need not represent the same biological processes.

The translational question is whether a GC-associated salivary transcriptomic signature can discriminate cases from controls. Do not equate retrospective case-control discrimination with early-stage screening utility. Original GEO data access recovered during the isolated cloud retry. Raw preprocessing of all four cohorts, differential expression and GO/GSEA are now completed. Model fitting and cohort-scale robustness are still pending. Both the original saved saliva outputs and new cloud-computed evidence are identified in the presentation provenance.

Python RMA and moderated DE have not been shown numerically equivalent to affy / limma.

RMA = robust multi-array average; BH = Benjamini-Hochberg; DE = differential expression.METHODS

Use the publication's terminology, while being precise about the independent implementation. PM-only raw RMA includes background correction, quantile normalization and summarization. Two-group empirical-Bayes moderated OLS borrows information across genes to stabilize residual variance, conceptually analogous to limma but not proven numerically identical.

All four completed normalization cohorts use raw-CEL-derived log2 expression without processed-matrix fallback. Four RuntimeWarnings indicate median polish reached its 50-iteration limit at tolerance 1e-6; logs do not identify probe sets or cohorts, and four warnings do not mean exactly four affected probe sets. Numerical affy parity remains unestablished. Filtering uses the global matrix median with declared alternatives. Mapping and implementation differences are plausible explanations, not proven causes of discrepancies. Saliva model candidates are discovered DEGs, not a silently imposed published panel.

Gastric cancer = stomach cancer. Source: Lopes et al., Introduction.INTRODUCTION

Open with the practical question, rather than the algorithms. Gastric cancer means cancer of the stomach. Endoscopy is an established way to investigate and diagnose stomach disease. Researchers would like easier ways to identify people who may benefit from further investigation.

Saliva is appealing because collection is non-invasive and can be self-performed. This study does not establish that a saliva test replaces endoscopy, detects cancer earlier, or improves survival. We are telling a research story, not recommending a medical test.

Transcriptomics = studying RNA signals. Activity changes, not DNA mutations.INTRODUCTION

Imagine DNA as an instruction book and RNA as copies of instructions being used. Gene expression is a way of measuring how actively genes are being used. Transcriptomics means examining many of these RNA signals together.

The activity bars are an analogy, not measured results. Lower measured activity does not mean a gene has disappeared, and a difference between groups does not establish that a gene causes cancer. The original public measurements were collected using microarrays; the reproduction did not newly sequence patients.

The important distinction is between a biological mirror and a statistical predictor. Saliva does not have to reproduce the stomach's exact gene changes to carry a potentially informative pattern of its own.

Lopes and colleagues used public data to explore both questions. This makes independent reanalysis possible. The importance is a patient-friendly research direction and a testable hypothesis, not proof of a clinically ready screening tool.

Completed: raw saliva processing and gene comparison. Models and robustness remain unassessed.METHOD

The first step is normalization: measurement cleanup, not making the biology of saliva and tissue identical. The reproduction processed all 94 raw saliva files through an independent Python implementation of RMA, without substituting a processed-data matrix.

A gene had to show a change greater than twofold in either direction and an adjusted P value below 0.05. The statistical adjustment accounts for testing many genes at once. This is a group comparison, not a diagnostic test.

The paper used a 70/30 training/test split and several machine-learning approaches. However, differing genes were identified using the full cohort before splitting, so test information could influence feature selection. We revisit that risk on slide 9. Independent Python processing is not proven numerically identical to the authors' R implementation.

The paper reported 1,302 differing genes in tissue: 579 with higher activity and 723 with lower activity in the cancer group. In saliva, it reported 14 differing genes, all with lower activity. None of those 14 appeared in the paper's selected tissue list.

That is evidence against a simple photocopy story, not proof that saliva has no connection to stomach disease. The paper also reported tissue biology related to cell division. Tissue gene comparisons, functional enrichment and overlap have not been run in the reproduction, so these remain published findings, not reproduced findings.

Source: paper, Table 3. AUC 0.868 does not mean 86.8% accuracy. Models not yet reproduced.PAPER RESULTS

AUC describes ranking: how often a person with cancer is ranked above a control. Around 0.5 is the chance-ranking benchmark; 1 is perfect ranking. It is not the percentage of correct predictions at a chosen threshold.

The paper reported all tissue models at around 0.5 when applied to saliva. A support vector machine built from the saliva genes scored 0.868, with a 95% confidence interval of 0.721-0.974. Its actual reported accuracy was 0.759, sensitivity 0.790 and specificity 0.700, also with wide intervals.

The saliva evaluation used a held-out part of the same 94-person cohort, not an independent external saliva cohort. Tissue models also varied on external tissue datasets: the paper's selected models scored 0.857 in the Chinese cohort and 0.913 in the Italian cohort. None of these model experiments has been run in the reproduction.

Sources: paper Results and computed saliva summary. Same cohort, not new patients. Partial reproduction.COMPARISON

This is the concrete result of the reproduction. All 94 raw saliva CEL files were processed. All 14 genes reported by the paper were recovered, with the same direction of change. But 23 additional genes met the declared thresholds, giving 37 rather than 14.

This is agreement between two analyses of the same public saliva cohort. It is not independent confirmation in new people. The gene universe and total selected list differ, so the honest verdict is partial reproduction, not exact numerical replication. These gene comparisons do not establish diagnostic accuracy.

The bars use the same zero-based scale: one gene is 20 SVG units in either row. The solid reproduced segment contains the 14 published genes; the hatched segment contains the 23 additional genes. The connecting line highlights identical set membership, not matching effect sizes.

09 / The limits of the evidenceNot yet assessed / prediction

A matching signal
is not a validated test.

Genes identified in the raw data

Genes kept after filtering

Similar starting counts do not guarantee the same selected genes.
Sources: REPORT.md and saliva summary. Both plots start at zero; differences are labeled, not magnified.COMPARISON

The reproduction mapped 20,953 genes versus 21,367 in the paper and retained 20,527 versus 20,407 after filtering. Possible explanations include updated gene names, duplicate-probe handling, filtering and independent statistical/preprocessing implementations. These are plausible explanations, not proven causes of the discrepancy.

Explain information leakage as seeing part of the answer before taking the test. The paper-like procedure identifies differing genes before the train/test split; this can make performance optimistic. Planned checks keep gene selection inside training, change the splits and shuffle labels to look for performance arising by chance. They have not been run on the full cohorts.

No independent external saliva cohort or validated early-stage screening performance was established. Software tests are not biological validation. "Not yet assessed" is an unknown, not a zero, a failed result, or evidence that the paper is incorrect.

Close with three distinct levels of evidence: a gene signal, trustworthy prediction and clinical evaluation. The reproduction assessed the first level only, and its agreement is partial. The paper's reported saliva model remains promising but needs independent validation and stronger safeguards.

A future validated saliva tool might help identify who needs further investigation. This study and reproduction do not establish that use. The goal of reproduction is a trustworthy assessment, not forcing the numbers to match. Scientific promise and clinical readiness are different.
